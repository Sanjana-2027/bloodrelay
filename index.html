CAN I DONATE?

✅ Who Can Donate Blood?

✅ YES - If:

• Age 18-65 years
• Weight 50kg+
• Healthy & Feeling Well
• Last donation 90+ days ago

❌ NO - Wait If:

• Fever in last 14 days
• Tattoo in last 6 months
• Pregnancy / Breastfeeding
• Alcohol in last 24 hrs

💡 After Donation:

• 10 mins rest
• Chala water tagandi
• Heavy lifting 24 hrs vaddhu
• Iron food - Palak, Dates
